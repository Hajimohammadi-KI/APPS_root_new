The missing S02 Stage B runtime is implemented: a reviewed, bounded comparison can deliver actual baseline or FSRS candidate dates on the English and German practice routes. The shipped declaration is empty, so ordinary schedules continue. No learner has been enrolled and no learning benefit has been measured.

The first supported comparison is deliberately finite: 2–12 already reviewed writing retrieval/retention tasks, each from a different construction, with both date policies represented and at most 30 days of follow-up. Each target contributes one original response. Speaking, unfamiliar material, automatic model judgments and broad scheduler activation remain outside this pilot.

The compiler requires an exact curriculum/task binding, independently approved human evaluation procedures, consented shadow history with at least two qualified delayed ratings per item, reproduced FSRS candidate dates, the preserved baseline dates and a prospectively reviewed comparison. Review findings cover allocation, outcomes, non-inferiority criteria, missing data, withdrawal, rollback and deployment. A bare approval flag or synthetic reviewer cannot compile a pilot.

The practice page requires an explicit opt-in. It saves the original progress snapshot before enabling the date overlay, records the selected arm and actual delivery date, and holds those constructions out of ordinary automatic recommendations until due. Manual topic choice remains available; intervening practice or exposure interrupts that target's comparison. Withdrawal removes the overlay before attempting to archive the withdrawal record, so a full storage quota cannot trap someone in the experiment. Expiry, unavailable plans and invalid bindings restore ordinary scheduling. Original events and new learner responses are retained.

Outcome analysis checks the original backup, enrollment and baseline, assigned dates, delivery records, first responses and current independent human assessments. Reviews written after the response window can assess original responses within that window. Missing responses, unsupported judgments, intervening practice and unknown withdrawal times remain separate from assessed successes and failures. Workload reporting counts delivered items and records observed writing time; clicks do not estimate total study time. Reports are descriptive and do not automatically qualify FSRS or award mastery.
