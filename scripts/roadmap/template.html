German program review

Prioritize recurring error patterns

Current evaluation can classify these errors, but the roadmap must also measure recurrence and repair by pattern. German practice now groups feedback into four focused loops instead of treating every correction as an isolated mistake.

Verben & Verbformen Subject agreement, conjugation, auxiliary and tense choices.
Kasus & Präpositionen Preposition government, article choice and case in one unit.
Rechtschreibung Spelling and capitalization stay meaningful, not cosmetic.
Adjektive & Endungen Adjective endings and other form-dependent endings.
